## `whoami`

I'm Atharv Pose, an IT undergraduate interested in the engineering behind the tools we use and the systems we depend on.

My work and experiments span **cybersecurity and OSINT, AI-assisted applications, computer vision, automation, web engineering, and hardware**. I enjoy projects that cross boundaries: turning scattered reconnaissance tools into one investigation workspace, turning drone footage into a 3D scene, or exploring what a small microcontroller can do in a handheld device.

I learn best by building. That means reading the documentation, trying the implementation, debugging the edge cases, and understanding why something works — not just getting it to run.

> **Build it. Understand it. Make it useful.**
